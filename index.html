On the desk without a login

These pages are included at $0. A field with no feed stays n/a.

Market

Index cards, headlines, and sector context. A context pill is not a signal. A delayed or cached quote is tagged.

News

A reader for the feed summary, a Why It's Moving note, and a link to the original.

JAMSEC

A read-only public page on the same $1–$50 and float under 40M lock. It is not a trading signal.

Signals

Price Spike Up and Price Spike Down at 5%, on stocks from $1–$50 with float under 40M. The preset cannot be changed.

Dilution

A dilution scorecard with a risk bar, a score from 0 to 100, and a PDF download.

Research

Stock Card and read-only research notes. This site does not publish the outcome of a study.

Squawk for the first 7 days

Browser-based Squawk is not on the no-login pages. A free account unlocks it for the first 7 days. After that, Squawk requires Paid and is ad-free. That window is only for Squawk. The paid subscription has no free trial. Squawk details

What Paid adds

Paid unlocks interactive filter controls, custom filtering, and saved filter presets, plus ad-free Squawk after the first 7 days. All of that is scoped per user account. See pricing

How the desk treats missing data

This site does not describe the data as universally connected or real-time. A delayed quote can wear a delayed tag. A cached value can wear a cached tag. A field with no feed stays n/a. A panel without a connected feed stays grey, and a feed that is failing stays marked that way.
