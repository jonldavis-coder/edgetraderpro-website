What is Edge Trader Pro?

Edge Trader Pro is a web desk for people who trade their own plan. The tagline is Your rules. Your data. Your desk. The current desk covers linked charts and workspaces, multi-pane screeners, the paper practice simulator, a trade journal, news and alerts, and rule and risk controls with research cards.

Is Edge Trader Pro investment advice?

Edge Trader Pro is not investment advice. Trading involves risk of loss. Past results do not guarantee future results. Nothing on this website is a recommendation to buy or sell. The desk is a process tool. It does not publish customer results.

Are the data feeds real-time and always connected?

No. This site does not describe the data as universally connected or real-time. A field with no feed stays n/a. A delayed quote can wear a delayed tag. A panel without a connected feed stays grey.

What can I do with charts and screeners?

Charts show candles, VWAP, volume, and MACD, with drawing tools and color links to screeners, news, and details. Workspaces save that layout. Screeners run in up to four panes, with built-in presets you can clone, filters, and watchlists you can import.

What is ProTrader MOSAIC?

ProTrader MOSAIC is the practice simulator. It is paper-only. You can replay a session and see paper fills on the chart. A paper fill is not a live order.

What does the journal include today?

Today the journal has editable rows and a reviews page for post-trade reviews. Broker execution import, automatic journal capture, chart marks for entry and exit, reports, a calendar, sharing and mentoring, and MAE/MFE analytics are on the roadmap. They are not in the current desk.

Will the desk place trades for me?

No. You use the desk yourself. The practice simulator does not send a live order. This website does not offer a service that buys or sells for you.

How do I get access, and what does it cost?

Pricing is not set. Request access or join the waitlist by emailing hello@edgetraderpro.com. Payment links are empty, so the buttons on the access page open that email.

Can you set the desk up for me?

Custom setup services are available. Contact us at hello@edgetraderpro.com. No price is listed for setup.

How do I reach support?

Email hello@edgetraderpro.com. Say what you were doing and what you expected. Do not send account numbers, passwords, or API keys. This site does not promise a response time.

Does the automated assistant send my question anywhere?

No. The automated assistant runs in your browser and answers from the text of this website only. It does not contact an outside service.
