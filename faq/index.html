Questions

Short answers on the free desk, Squawk, paid plans, and data. EdgeDashPro is not investment advice. Trading involves risk of loss. Past results do not guarantee future results.

What is EdgeDashPro?

EdgeDashPro is a web desk for people who trade their own plan. The tagline is Your rules. Your data. Your desk. There is a free public tier, and a paid tier for a user account.

Is EdgeDashPro investment advice?

EdgeDashPro is not investment advice. Trading involves risk of loss. Past results do not guarantee future results. Nothing on this website is a recommendation to buy or sell. The desk is a process tool. It does not publish customer results.

What is on the free public tier?

The free public tier is $0. It does not require a login, and it is ad-supported. It is read-only: Market, News, JAMSEC, Signals, Dilution, Stock Card, and Research. Every free public view is locked server-side to $1–$50 only and float under 40M. Signals is locked to Price Spike Up and Price Spike Down at 5% on those stocks. Free visitors see only those locked presets. They cannot change or customize filters. Interactive filter controls and saved filter presets are paid. Browser-based Squawk is not on those pages.

What can I change on Signals and the other free views?

Every free public view is locked server-side to $1–$50 only and float under 40M. Signals is locked to Price Spike Up and Price Spike Down at 5% on those stocks. Free visitors see only those locked presets. They cannot change or customize filters. Interactive filter controls and saved filter presets are paid. Paid is $29/month or $297/year for a limited time, regularly $69/month or $497/year. There is no free trial of the subscription. Paid is what unlocks the interactive filter controls.

How does the Squawk trial work?

Free users must create a free account to access the 7-day Squawk trial. That account unlocks browser-based Squawk for the first 7 days. Market, News, JAMSEC, Dilution, Stock Card, and Research stay on the free tier with no login. After 7 days, Squawk requires the paid tier at $29/month or $297/year, and paid Squawk is ad-free. The 7-day window is only for Squawk. The paid subscription has no free trial and goes straight to Stripe Checkout.

What does the paid tier unlock?

Paid is $29/month or $297/year for a limited time, regularly $69/month or $497/year. There is no free trial of the subscription. Checkout goes straight to Stripe. Paid unlocks the interactive filter controls, custom filtering, saved filter presets, and ad-free Squawk. Each of those is scoped to the user account. Free visitors cannot change the locked presets. Ad-free Squawk is what you need after the first 7 days.

Are the data feeds real-time and always connected?

No. This site does not describe the data as universally connected or real-time. A delayed quote can wear a delayed tag. A cached value can wear a cached tag. A field with no feed stays n/a. A panel without a connected feed stays grey, and a feed that is failing stays marked that way.

Will the desk place trades for me?

No. You use the desk yourself. EdgeDashPro does not buy or sell for you. It is not a brokerage. This website does not offer a service that buys or sells for you.

How do I get access, and what does it cost?

The free public tier is $0 and needs no checkout. Paid is $29/month or $297/year for a limited time, regularly $69/month or $497/year. There is no free trial of the subscription. Questions about a plan go to hello@edgedashpro.com.

Does the free tier show ads?

Yes. The free public pages are ad-supported. Browser-based Squawk is a 7-day trial on a free account, not a no-login page. After 7 days, Squawk requires Paid and is ad-free. The paid subscription has no free trial. Terms, Privacy, and the risk disclosure apply.

How do I reach support?

Email hello@edgedashpro.com. Say what you were doing and what you expected. Do not send account numbers, passwords, or API keys. This site does not promise a response time.

Does the automated assistant send my question anywhere?

No. The automated assistant runs in your browser and answers from the text of this website only. It does not contact an outside service.
