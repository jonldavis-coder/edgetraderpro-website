Market

Market is on the free public tier. No login. The view is context, not a signal, and not a record of results.

What the page shows

Market stacks index cards, market headlines, a theme heat map, a sector ETF heat map, and industry tiles for pharma, biotech, semiconductors, regional banks, oil and gas, and gold miners. A bull and bear ETF tape sits with them.

A tile can show Favors shorts, Favors longs, or Neutral. That pill is context, not a signal. A change under half a percent reads Neutral. Bear funds flip the read, because a rise in the bear fund means the sector is falling.

Tags, and fields that stay n/a

This site does not describe the data as universally connected or real-time. A delayed quote can wear a delayed tag. A cached value can wear a cached tag. A field with no feed stays n/a. A panel without a connected feed stays grey, and a feed that is failing stays marked that way.

The filter lock

Every free public view is locked server-side to $1–$50 only and float under 40M. Signals is locked to Price Spike Up and Price Spike Down at 5% on those stocks. Free visitors see only those locked presets. They cannot change or customize filters. Interactive filter controls and saved filter presets are paid. Names outside that band are not opened up from the browser on the free tier.

Interactive filter controls, custom filtering, and saved filter presets are paid, and they are stored on the user account. See pricing.
