Features

Market, News, JAMSEC, Signals, Dilution, Stock Card, and Research are $0, need no login, and are ad-supported. A free account unlocks browser-based Squawk for the first 7 days. Paid is $29/month or $297/year for a limited time, regularly $69/month or $497/year. There is no free trial of the subscription.

Free public tier

Market, News, JAMSEC, Signals, Dilution, Stock Card, and Research are read-only. You do not sign in for those pages. Standard Terms, Privacy, and the risk disclosure apply. EdgeDashPro is not investment advice. Trading involves risk of loss. Past results do not guarantee future results.

Market

Market is a public page: index cards, market headlines, a theme heat map, a sector ETF heat map, and industry tiles for pharma, biotech, semiconductors, regional banks, oil and gas, and gold miners. A bull and bear ETF tape sits with them. A tile can show Favors shorts, Favors longs, or Neutral. That pill is context, not a signal. A change under half a percent reads Neutral. Bear funds flip the read, because a rise in the bear fund means the sector is falling.

A tile can show a delayed tag when the quote is delayed, or a cached tag when the value is cached. Market page

News

News is a public page. A headline opens a reader with the feed summary, a Why It's Moving note, and a link to the original. The free tier does not add custom news filters. A field with no feed stays n/a.

JAMSEC

JAMSEC is a public page on the free desk. It is read-only and uses the same $1–$50 and float under 40M lock as the rest of that tier. A field with no feed stays n/a. This site does not describe JAMSEC as a trading signal or as advice.

Signals

Signals is a free public page. It is locked to Price Spike Up and Price Spike Down at 5%, and only on stocks from $1–$50 with float under 40M. The same $1–$50 and float under 40M lock applies on every free public view. A free visitor cannot change that preset. A price spike on this page is not investment advice. A field with no feed stays n/a, and a delayed or cached value can wear that tag.

Dilution, Stock Card, and Research

Dilution, Stock Card, and Research are public, read-only pages. Dilution can show a risk bar and a score from 0 to 100, with offering math, and a PDF download. The stock card shows the symbol you open. Research notes stay on the page. Cells without a feed stay grey or n/a. A connected feed that is failing stays marked as failing. Research page

Browser-based Squawk

Squawk is not one of the no-login pages. Free users must create a free account to access the 7-day Squawk trial. That account unlocks browser-based Squawk for the first 7 days. Squawk can be switched on or off, with a volume control, and it reads chosen headlines. It does not read a feed that is missing as if the item were present.

After 7 days, Squawk requires the paid tier at $29/month or $297/year, and paid Squawk is ad-free, scoped to the user account. The 7-day window is only for Squawk. The paid subscription has no free trial.

Locked presets

Every free public view is locked server-side to $1–$50 only and float under 40M. Signals is locked to Price Spike Up and Price Spike Down at 5% on those stocks. Free visitors see only those locked presets. They cannot change or customize filters. Interactive filter controls and saved filter presets are paid. The lock is applied on the server. The free desk does not offer a control that changes the price band, the float band, or the 5% spike preset, and it does not save a preset.

Paid tier

Paid Monthly is $29/month for a limited time, regularly $69/month. Paid Annual is $297/year for a limited time, regularly $497/year. There is no free trial of the subscription. Questions about a plan go to hello@edgedashpro.com.

Paid unlocks, each scoped per user account:

  • Interactive filter controls, custom filtering, and saved filter presets.
  • Ad-free Squawk, required after the first 7 days.

This site does not describe the data as universally connected or real-time. A delayed quote can wear a delayed tag. A cached value can wear a cached tag. A field with no feed stays n/a. A panel without a connected feed stays grey, and a feed that is failing stays marked that way.
