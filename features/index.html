Current desk

Features

The areas below are the current desk, taken from the product release log through 2 November 2026. The public sign-in page showed this mark on 7 November 2026. Screens behind sign-in were not re-checked for this page. Nothing here is a performance claim.

Linked charts and workspaces

Open a chart on the dark quote layout: company and last price, candles, VWAP, volume, and MACD. Drawing tools draw on the chart. A color link ties that chart to scanner panes, news, and the details window sharing the color.

A workspace saves the chart layout, the scanner panes, link colors, signal and news colors, and squawk switches. You can save, save as, rename, duplicate, delete, set a default, and export or import the workspace as JSON. On an iPad the chart grid draws tighter and leaves the saved layout as it is.

Charts and screeners in more detail

Saved multi-pane screeners

Run one, two, three, or four scanner panes. Two sit side by side, three are columns, and four is a two-by-two grid. On iPad, two and three stack, and four stays a grid. Each pane keeps its own preset, filters, tab, sort, and cooldown.

Thirty-eight built-in screen presets ship in named groups, including low float, premarket and postmarket, and fundamental screens. Clone and edit a preset. Saving a copy does not replace a built-in screen or a scanner you already saved. Scanners save on the signed-in account. The first visit can copy scanners that lived only in that browser, without replacing a copy already saved.

When the data is present, presets can use price, change, gap, market cap, float, relative volume, volume, price times volume, average volume, ADR, premarket and postmarket change and volume, one-week, one-month, and one-year performance, and SMA, EMA, ADX, and Stochastic when daily bars are long enough, plus common stock and exchange. P/E, PEG, dividends, IPO date, depositary receipt, and technical rating stay on the preset as n/a when there is no feed. Desk risk rules do not block a scan.

Watchlists

Import a symbol list. A TradingView export is accepted. US equity prefixes are stripped, and section headers, indices, futures, forex, and crypto are dropped. Replace the main list or merge into it. Dated lists stay selectable. A name outside the price or float band can remain on the list and show an out-of-band tag when a quote or float is already cached.

ProTrader MOSAIC, the practice simulator

The practice simulator is paper-only and includes replay. Read the practice page for what that screen does, and what it does not do.

Journal and reviews

The journal has rows you can edit. A separate reviews page holds post-trade reviews. Further journal tools are labeled on the roadmap and are not in the product today. See the journal page.

News, alerts, squawk, and market context

News rows include a second pane with its own filters. A headline opens an in-app reader with the feed summary, a Why It's Moving note, and a link to the original. Squawk reads chosen headlines and market alerts aloud: an on/off switch, a volume slider, a test, and settings for voice, rate, and which items are read. A close back under VWAP can be spoken as Lost VWAP, and a close back above it as Reclaimed VWAP. VWAP squawk starts off until you change it.

Alerts has an inbox, a dilution feed, and rules. You can filter alert rows and create a rule. A signal is an alert row, not a trade. Halt up, halt down, and halt/resume are separate types. Each signal type and each news source can take a color, shown as a light tint and a solid bar.

Market overview stacks index cards, market-moving headlines, a theme heat map, a sector ETF heat map, and industry tiles for pharma, biotech, semiconductors, regional banks, oil and gas, and gold miners. A bull and bear ETF tape sits with them. A tile can show Favors shorts, Favors longs, or Neutral. That pill is context, not a signal. A change under half a percent reads Neutral. Bear funds flip the read, because a rise in the bear fund means the sector is falling. A tile can show a delayed tag when the quote is delayed.

Rules, research cards, and dilution

Risk rules are visible on the desk, including the price band, float, share cap, name cap, daily loss limit, a stop on every trade, no short without a borrow, regular hours, and a named setup. Research cards cover a stock card and a dilution scorecard. Details are on the rules and risk page.

Also on the desk

  • Sign-in carries the e/T pro mark. A long idle period signs you out.
  • An admin can switch the desk to a demo tape. Demo adds a banner, and the desk asks before the switch.
  • Mission Control opens a desk overview: session gauges, a pre-flight list, and tiles to the rest of the desk. A status board reports component health. A feed that is not configured stays grey. Status can use a three-screen console view. The choice is remembered in this browser.
  • An earnings view can filter on market cap. Names with no market cap drop out while that filter is on.
  • Level 2 and time and sales are on the desk. Borrow and locate, the flow panel, a short-interest cell, and some social mentions stay grey until that feed is connected.
  • kCURVE, a signed-in training library, holds a curated reading list and the playbook. Those files open only after sign-in.

This site does not describe the data as universally connected or real-time. Where a feed is missing, the desk shows n/a or stays grey.
