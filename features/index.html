Features

Market, News, JAMSEC, Dilution, Stock Card, and Research are $0, need no login, and are ad-supported. A free account unlocks browser-based Squawk for the first 7 days. Paid is $29.99/month or $259/year, a 25-user launch, with no free trial of the subscription. Nothing here is a performance claim.

Free public tier

Market, News, JAMSEC, Dilution, Stock Card, and Research are a strict read-only experience. You do not sign in for those pages. Standard Terms, Privacy, and the risk disclosure apply. Market

Market is a public page: index cards, market headlines, a theme heat map, a sector ETF heat map, and industry tiles for pharma, biotech, semiconductors, regional banks, oil and gas, and gold miners. A bull and bear ETF tape sits with them. A tile can show Favors shorts, Favors longs, or Neutral. That pill is context, not a signal. A change under half a percent reads Neutral. Bear funds flip the read, because a rise in the bear fund means the sector is falling.

A tile can show a delayed tag when the quote is delayed, or a cached tag when the value is cached. Market page

News

News is a public page. A headline opens a reader with the feed summary, a Why It's Moving note, and a link to the original. The free tier does not add custom news filters. A field with no feed stays n/a.

JAMSEC

JAMSEC is a public page on the free desk. It is read-only and uses the same locked filters as the rest of that tier. A field with no feed stays n/a. This site does not describe JAMSEC as a signal or as advice.

Dilution, Stock Card, and Research

Dilution, Stock Card, and Research are public, read-only pages. Dilution can show a risk bar and a score from 0 to 100, with offering math, and a PDF download. The stock card shows the symbol you open. Research notes stay on the page. Cells without a feed stay grey or n/a. A connected feed that is failing stays marked as failing. Research page

Browser-based Squawk

Squawk is not one of the no-login pages. Free users must create a free account to access the 7-day Squawk trial. That account unlocks browser-based Squawk for the first 7 days. Squawk can be switched on or off, with a volume control, and it reads chosen headlines and market alerts. It does not read a feed that is missing as if the item were present.

After 7 days, Squawk requires the paid tier at $29.99/month or $259/year, and paid Squawk is ad-free, scoped to the user account. The 7-day window is only for Squawk. The paid subscription has no free trial.

Locked filters

Filters on the free tier are locked server-side to $1–$50 only and float under 40M. There are no custom filters and no saved presets on that tier. The lock is applied on the server. The free desk does not offer a control that widens the price band or the float band, and it does not save a preset.

Paid tier

Paid Monthly is $29.99/month. Paid Annual is $259/year. There is no free trial. Checkout goes straight to Stripe. The paid launch holds 25 users. Until a Stripe Payment Link is filled in, the buttons on the pricing page email hello@edgetraderpro.com.

Paid unlocks three things, each scoped per user account:

  • Custom filtering and saved filter presets.
  • The journal: a unified trade journal, a P&L calendar, and post-trade insight cards and reviews. See the journal page.
  • Ad-free Squawk, required after the first 7 days.

This site does not describe the data as universally connected or real-time. A delayed quote can wear a delayed tag. A cached value can wear a cached tag. A field with no feed stays n/a. A panel without a connected feed stays grey, and a feed that is failing stays marked that way.
