Privacy

Draft. This is a working notice for the marketing site, not a final privacy policy.

EdgeDashPro can be reached at hello@edgedashpro.com. This page describes the marketing website, and it names how the product tiers differ.

What this site collects

You do not create an account on this marketing site. Market, News, JAMSEC, Dilution, Stock Card, and Research do not require a login. Browser-based Squawk does: a free account in the product unlocks it for the first 7 days, and after that Squawk requires a paid account. That account is part of the product, not a form on these pages. If you email us, we receive what you chose to send, including your address and the message. Do not send account numbers, passwords, or API keys.

This marketing site does not run its own analytics product and does not sell personal information. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may keep ordinary request logs. This notice does not control the host's own logging.

Ads on the free tier

The free public pages are ad-supported. They do not require a login. Browser-based Squawk is separate: a free account unlocks it for the first 7 days, and after that Squawk requires the paid tier and is ad-free. This marketing site does not set its own advertising cookie. This draft does not name an ad network. The notice will name it when one is in use.

The automated assistant

The assistant runs in your browser. Your question is matched against text that shipped with this website. The page does not send that question to another service.

Checkout

Paid checkout is handled by Stripe in the app. There is no free trial of the subscription. This marketing site does not store a card number. Questions about a plan go to hello@edgedashpro.com.

Cookies

This marketing site does not set a tracking cookie for advertising. Your browser may store nothing from us. The host may use its own technical cookies.

Children

This site is not directed at children.

Changes

When this draft is replaced, the new notice will be posted on this page. Questions: hello@edgedashpro.com.
