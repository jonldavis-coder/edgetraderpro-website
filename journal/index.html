Journal

A journal of the trades you record, with rows you can edit and a place to write the review after the trade.

What is in the journal today

  • A trade journal on the desk.
  • Rows with an edit action, so a note can be corrected after you enter it.
  • A reviews page for a post-trade review: what you planned, what you did, and what you would repeat.

The journal stores the row you write. This website does not turn those rows into a profit figure, a win rate, or a public track record.

On the roadmap

Not in the current desk

These journal additions are listed so the direction is clear. They are not available. They are not part of the product described above.

  • Broker execution import. Bring fills in from a broker instead of typing each one. Not in the current desk.
  • Automatic journal capture. The journal records the trade as you complete it, so the row is not typed by hand. Not in the current desk.
  • Entry and exit marked on charts. The prices you entered and exited, drawn on the price chart. Not in the current desk.
  • Reports by setup, tag, time, and price. Group the journal and read it back by how you traded. Not in the current desk.
  • A journal calendar. See which days have journal rows, on a month view. Not in the current desk.
  • Sharing and mentoring. Let a mentor read a journal you choose to share. Not in the current desk.
  • MAE/MFE and exit-efficiency analytics. How far price moved against the trade and for the trade, and how much of that favorable move the exit kept. Not in the current desk.
