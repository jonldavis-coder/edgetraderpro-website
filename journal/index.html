Journal

The journal is a paid unlock. It is not on the free public tier. It is scoped to the user account.

What the paid journal includes

  • A unified trade journal of the trades you record.
  • A P&L calendar of those rows.
  • Post-trade insight cards and reviews: what you planned, what you did, and what you would repeat.

Paid Monthly is $29.99/month and Paid Annual is $259/year, with no free trial of the subscription. The same account holds custom filtering, saved filter presets, and ad-free Squawk. Browser-based Squawk before that is a 7-day trial on a free account, not a trial of this journal. Pricing and access

The journal stores the row you write. This website does not turn those rows into a public track record, a win rate, or a promise about the next trade.
