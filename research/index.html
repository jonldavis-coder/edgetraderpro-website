Research

Dilution, Stock Card, and Research are on the free public tier. No login. A page of research is not a recommendation.

Dilution

The dilution scorecard can show a risk bar and a score from 0 to 100, with offering math. Download a PDF of the card. A field with no feed stays n/a.

Stock Card

The stock card is the read-only card for the symbol you open. Cells without a feed stay grey. A connected feed that is failing stays marked as failing.

Research notes

Research can hold a short case, a squeeze case, and a plan. This site does not publish the outcome of any study. Filters on the free tier are locked server-side to $1–$50 only and float under 40M. There are no custom filters and no saved presets on that tier.

Tags

This site does not describe the data as universally connected or real-time. A delayed quote can wear a delayed tag. A cached value can wear a cached tag. A field with no feed stays n/a. A panel without a connected feed stays grey, and a feed that is failing stays marked that way.

Custom filtering of this research, and any saved preset, belongs to the paid account. The free pages stay on the locked band.
