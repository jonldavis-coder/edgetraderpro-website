ProTrader MOSAIC (practice simulator)

A place to rehearse a session. Replay is part of the simulator. Nothing on this screen is a live order.

ProTrader MOSAIC is paper-only. A paper fill drawn on the chart is practice. It is not an order sent to a broker, and it is not a record of profit or loss.

What you can practice

The simulator opens as its own part of the desk and can pop out to a window. The chart can show candles, volume, VWAP, and an EMA, plus estimated limit-up and limit-down bands, paper fills, and order lines. You can switch chart styles. That choice is saved in this browser. Saving the layout writes the windows.

Replay plays a session back so you can review price action away from the moment it printed. Replay depends on the session that is available. This page does not claim that every symbol, every day, or a live tape is connected.

What it is not

  • It does not send a live order.
  • It is not a brokerage account and this website shows no account number.
  • It is not a promise about future trades. Edge Trader Pro is not investment advice. Trading involves risk of loss. Past results do not guarantee future results.
