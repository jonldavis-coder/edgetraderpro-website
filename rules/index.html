Rules & Risk

The desk shows the rules around a trade. A rule on a screen does not remove market risk.

Rules the desk shows

These limits are part of the desk. They apply to trading from the desk. They do not block a scan. They are not a forecast.

  • $1 to $20. The working price band for names the desk treats as in range.
  • Float under 20 million shares. Names at or above that float can still sit on a watchlist with an out-of-band tag.
  • A share cap. Size is capped on the order.
  • A cap on open names. The desk limits how many names are open at once, with a tighter cap on a Federal Reserve announcement day.
  • A daily loss limit. The day has a loss figure you can see. It is a limit, not a result.
  • A stop on every trade. The rule calls for a stop with the order.
  • No borrow, no short. A short needs a borrow.
  • Regular trading hours only. Extended-hours orders are outside the rule.
  • A named setup. The trade is tied to a setup you can name.

Levered products on the bull and bear tape are marked so they stay out of a trade. The tape itself is context, not a signal.

Research cards and dilution

A stock card and a dilution scorecard reopen the last symbol for the account unless the address bar names one. Dilution shows a risk bar and a score from 0 to 100, with offering math. Download a PDF of either card.

A research write-up can hold a short case, a squeeze case, and a plan. This site does not publish the outcome of any study.

Short interest, borrow and locate, and some other research cells stay grey until a feed is connected. A connected feed that is failing stays marked as failing. Level 2 and time and sales are separate from those grey cells.
