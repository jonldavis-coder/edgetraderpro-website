Charts & Screeners

Charts, screeners, and watchlists stay linked by color, and the layout can be saved with the account.

The chart

The desk chart and the details window use the dark quote layout: company and last price, a tab row, drawing tools, and candles with VWAP, volume, and MACD. A popped-out chart uses the same layout. A tab or field with no feed stays n/a. Demo chart bars are a real intraday series for that sample, and the details close uses the same prior close as the headline.

A color dot on a scanner pane links that pane to charts, news, and details of the same color. A ticker in a headline, written as a symbol or an exchange prefix, can open details.

Workspaces

Workspace, in the top bar, saves the chart layout, scanner panes, link colors, signal and news colors, and squawk switches. The actions are save, save as, rename, duplicate, delete, set default, and a JSON export and import. Create, rename, duplicate, and delete ask first and change only your copies.

Multi-pane screeners

Pick one, two, three, or four panes. Two sit side by side, three are columns, four is a two-by-two grid. iPad stacks two and three and keeps four as a grid. Each pane has its own preset, including filters, tab, sort, and cooldown, and panes on the same tab share one poll.

Thirty-eight built-in screens are scanner presets. They keep their names, filters, and sort, in groups that include low float, premarket and postmarket, and fundamental screens. You can clone and edit a preset. A saved copy does not replace a built-in screen.

Filters, and fields that stay n/a

Presets apply price, change, gap, market cap, float, relative volume, volume, price times volume, average volume, ADR, premarket and postmarket change and volume, and one-week, one-month, and one-year performance. SMA, EMA, ADX, and Stochastic apply when daily bars are long enough. Common stock and exchange apply too. P/E, PEG, dividends, IPO date, depositary receipt, and technical rating remain n/a. There is no feed for those fields.

Market-cap filters accept spellings such as 300, 300M, or 0.3B for 300 million dollars, and hide names above that cap. Float accepts a spelling such as 20M. Signal filters are checkboxes, one per concept, remembered in the browser until you change them.

Watchlists

Import a symbol list, including a list exported from TradingView. You can replace the main list or merge symbols into it. Each imported name can be kept as its own dated list, and older dated lists stay selectable. Names outside the $1 to $20 band, or with float of at least 20 million shares, can stay on the list and show an out-of-band tag when a quote or float is already on hand.

The same saved scanner list is available on each scanner pane, on charts, on signals, on squawk, and on the news scan menu.
