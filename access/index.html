Pricing

Pricing and access

Public pages are free and need no login. Every free view is locked to $1–$50 and float under 40M, and Signals is locked to Price Spike Up and Price Spike Down at 5%. Visitors cannot change those filters. A free account unlocks browser-based Squawk for the first 7 days. After that, Squawk requires Paid and is ad-free. Continue to Paid Monthly ($29.99/month) and Continue to Paid Annual ($259/year) open https://app.edgedashpro.com/pricing, straight to Stripe Checkout, with no free trial of the subscription.
